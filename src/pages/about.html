🚀 Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

1. Market Data Collection

Hum Binance Futures se real-time market data collect karte hain — price, volume, RSI, MACD, EMA, ADX, ATR waghera.

2. Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Aapke chune hue timeframe ke saath saath higher timeframes (1h, 4h, 1d) ka bhi analysis hota hai taake macro trend clear ho.

3. AI Processing

Data Groq AI (primary) aur Google Gemini (fallback) ko bhej diya jata hai. AI market structure, candlestick patterns, indicator confluence, aur news impact ko analyze karta hai.

4. Quality Gates

Signal generate karne se pehle multiple quality checks hote hain:

  • ADX check (trend strength)
  • RSI zone check (overbought/oversold)
  • HTF conflict check
  • Risk:Reward ratio check
  • Overextension check

5. Final Signal

Sirf high-quality signals aapko bhejta hai — Direction (LONG/SHORT), Entry, Stop Loss, Take Profit, Confidence, aur Reasoning ke saath.
