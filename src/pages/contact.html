❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Signal generate nahi ho raha, kya karun?
A: Pehle check karein ke aapka daily quota bacha hai ya nahi. Agar quota khatam hai to kal reset hoga ya premium lein. Agar quota hai phir bhi issue ho to WhatsApp par contact karein.
Q: Signal kitna accurate hota hai?
A: Hamara AI-powered signal average 60-70% win rate deta hai. App mein "Signal Accuracy" section mein real stats dekh sakte hain.
Q: Premium kaise milega?
A: Do tareeqe hain: (1) WhatsApp par contact karke payment karein, (2) Premium code redeem karein jo hum bhejte hain.
Q: Referral code se kya hota hai?
A: Jab aap apna referral code doosron ko dete hain aur woh register karte hain, to aapko +2 bonus signals milte hain (max 10 referrals = +20 signals). Yeh bonus permanent hota hai.
Q: Password bhool gaya, kya karun?
A: Filhal password reset feature nahi hai. WhatsApp par contact karein aur hum manually reset kar denge.
Q: Device lock kya hai?
A: Ek device par sirf ek account ban sakta hai. Yeh security feature hai taake log fake accounts na bana sakein.
Q: Withdraw/deposit kaise karein?
A: CryptoWorld signals sirf deta hai, trading aap khud apne Binance account se karte hain. Hum aapka paisa hold nahi karte.
Q: App free hai?
A: Haan, basic version 100% free hai (1 signal/day). Premium plans optional hain agar aap zyada signals chahte hain.
